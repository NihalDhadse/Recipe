Spicy Chicken Curry

  • Wash and cut chicken into medium pieces.
  • Heat oil, sauté onions until golden brown.
  • Add tomatoes, ginger-garlic paste, and cook until soft.
  • Mix in turmeric, chili, coriander, and garam masala.
  • Add chicken pieces and stir well.
  • Cook covered until chicken is tender and curry thickens.

Crispy Fish Fry

  • Clean fish fillets and pat them dry.
  • Marinate with turmeric, chili powder, and lemon juice.
  • Let it rest for 20 minutes.
  • Coat each fillet lightly with rice flour.
  • Shallow fry in hot oil until golden brown.
  • Serve hot with onion slices and green chutney.

Mutton Biryani

  • Marinate mutton with curd, ginger-garlic paste, and spices.
  • Soak basmati rice for 30 minutes.
  • Fry onions until golden and keep aside.
  • Cook marinated mutton until tender.
  • Layer rice and mutton with fried onions and ghee.
  • Cover and dum cook for 20 minutes for rich flavor.

Grilled Chicken Wings

  • Wash and clean chicken wings.
  • Marinate with BBQ sauce, garlic, pepper, and olive oil.
  • Let it rest for 1–2 hours for deep flavor.
  • Preheat grill and place wings on medium heat.
  • Grill both sides until crispy and slightly charred.
  • Serve hot with garlic dip or mayo.

Prawn Masala

  • Clean and devein prawns.
  • Heat oil, add onions and sauté until golden.
  • Add tomatoes, chili, and turmeric powder.
  • Mix in prawns and cook on medium heat.
  • Add coconut milk for richness.
  • Garnish with coriander and serve with rice.

Egg Keema Curry

  • Boil eggs and grate them coarsely.
  • Sauté onions, ginger, and garlic in oil.
  • Add tomatoes and cook until soft.
  • Mix in chili, turmeric, and garam masala.
  • Add grated egg and stir gently.
  • Simmer for 5 minutes and serve hot with roti.
